⚖️ Mandatory Compliance — Solid Waste Management Rules, 2016

Under the Government of India's Solid Waste Management Rules, institutions are required to provide hygienic disposal facilities for sanitary waste. Lyra Incinerators are CPCB-compliant and meet all prescribed standards, making compliance seamless for your institution.
